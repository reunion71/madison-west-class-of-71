1971 Yearbook Archives (class of '71 and more)

To find a specific person or subject in the archives, follow these steps:

  1. Update your search: After selecting the "Search the Archives" button, replace the default name "John Smith" with the specific name or topic you want to find.
  2. Run the search: Click the yellow Explore the Archives → button.
  3. Open the results: Select The Madison West High School Yearbooks from the search results.
  4. Navigate multiple results: If your search returns more than one result, use the Search Result 1 / X > > button at the top of the page to cycle through them.

Support the West Digital Archive

West has been working on building a digital archive of its history. It is still in progress, but you can check it out at bit.ly/westdigitalarchives.

If you have enjoyed or benefitted from our digital archive, we would love your help!

Do you have West memorabilia or artifacts that might be a good addition to our archive? Please reach out to see if we could use your donation! We are especially in need of yearbooks from before 1970. We are also happy to return items after we are done digitizing if you prefer.

The digitization and hosting of our archive is not without cost. Consider making a monetary gift so that we can continue to digitize and upload more of our shared history.

For questions about donating materials or making a monetary gift, please visit the Madison West High School Digital Archive contact page.
