Mobile Devices — Open PDF

On a phone or tablet, tap “Mobile Devices” to open Part 1 of the yearbook in a new tab. The PDF will not load on this page automatically.

To search the yearbook, click inside the PDF viewer and press Ctrl + F on Windows or Command + F on Mac. If the viewer does not display, open the PDF in a new tab.
